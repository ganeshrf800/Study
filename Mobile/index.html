Filter technologies

Why one phone needs four kinds of acoustic and ceramic filters.

SAW≈ 0.4 – 1.6 GHz
Interdigital transducers on LiTaO₃ or LiNbO₃. Cheap and small. Used for low-band duplexers, GNSS and receive-only filters.
TC-SAW / IHP-SAW≈ 0.6 – 2.7 GHz
Oxide overlay or layered substrate cuts drift to a few ppm/°C. Needed where duplex gaps are narrow, as in B13, B26 and n71.
BAW (SMR / FBAR)≈ 1.5 – 7 GHz
Bulk resonance in a thin AlN or ScAlN film. High Q and steep skirts for B25, n41, n7 and Wi-Fi coexistence. Broadcom FBAR; Qorvo and Qualcomm SMR.
LTCC / IPD / XBAW≈ 3 – 8 GHz
Wide fractional bandwidth for n77 (900 MHz wide) and n79. LTCC and IPD trade Q for bandwidth; XBAW-type resonators bridge the gap.
