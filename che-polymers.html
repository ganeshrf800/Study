Polymers (NCERT Chemistry Part II, Ch 15)

Addition and condensation polymers, rubber and plastics

A polymer is a very large molecule built by joining many small monomers. In addition polymerisation unsaturated monomers add to one another with no loss of atoms; in condensation polymerisation bifunctional monomers join with the loss of a small molecule such as water. The structure of the chain decides whether the material is a plastic, a fibre or an elastomer.

  • Addition polymers: polythene, PVC, polystyrene, Teflon, Orlon (polyacrylonitrile), natural rubber, neoprene. Condensation polymers: nylon-6,6, nylon-6, terylene (PET), Bakelite.
  • LDPE (branched) is made by free-radical polymerisation at 350-570 K and 1000-2000 atm with a trace of oxygen or peroxide; HDPE (linear, denser) is made with a Ziegler-Natta catalyst (TiCl4 + Al(C2H5)3) at 333-343 K and 6-7 atm.
  • Nylon-6,6 = hexane-1,6-diamine + hexanedioic (adipic) acid, losing two H2O per repeat unit. Bakelite = phenol + methanal, cross-linked 3-D network, thermosetting.
  • By force: elastomers (weak forces; rubber), fibres (strong H-bonds; nylon, terylene), thermoplastics (soften on heating; polythene, PVC), thermosetting plastics (cross-linked; Bakelite, melamine).
  • Natural rubber is cis-1,4-polyisoprene. Vulcanisation heats it with sulphur (373-415 K) to form S cross-links, making it harder and more elastic. Biodegradable polymers include PHBV and nylon-2-nylon-6.

In the real world

A 1-litre PET bottle weighs roughly 25 g. The repeat unit has M = 192.17 g/mol, so 25 g is 25 / 192.17 = 0.130 mol of units, or 0.130 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 7.8 × 10²² repeat units. Much of India's used PET is collected by the informal waste sector and recycled into polyester fibre for clothing and filling. For nylon-6,6, 1 kg of polymer needs 1000 × 262.35 / 226.32 = 1159 g of monomers and releases 159 g of water. The single-use plastic ban of July 2022 covered items such as plastic cutlery, ear buds with plastic sticks and thin carry bags, pushing industry to biodegradable alternatives like PHBV. Truck and car tyres combine vulcanised natural rubber and Buna-S, with nylon-6 or polyester cords for strength.

Which statement about nylon-6,6 is correct?

Nylon-6,6 forms from hexane-1,6-diamine and hexanedioic acid by condensation, releasing two molecules of water per repeat unit. It is a linear polyamide fibre, not cross-linked like Bakelite.
