1 · Standby powerbattery or adapter present
The EC and always-on rails are live. The EC watches the power button, lid and charger.
2 · Power sequencing≈ 10–50 ms
On a button press the EC switches on the system rails in order and releases the SoC from reset.
3 · Root of trustsecurity engine
CSME or PSP boots from ROM, loads its firmware from SPI flash and verifies the first UEFI code (Boot Guard).
4 · Memory trainingPEI · FSP-M
UEFI trains the LPDDR5X interface. Results are cached so later boots skip most of it.
5 · Device discoveryDXE
PCIe is enumerated, the NVMe and graphics (GOP) drivers load, USB and the keyboard come up.
6 · Secure BootBDS
The signed Windows Boot Manager is loaded from the SSD's EFI partition; each stage is measured into the TPM.
7 · OS hand-offkernel
Windows reads ACPI tables, loads drivers, unlocks BitLocker from the TPM and starts the desktop.
