Year 1 means the first year of compulsory primary school (US Grade 1, CBSE Class 1, Singapore Primary 1), roughly age 6–7. Topics are highlights, not full syllabi.

All three systems are mid-reform: CBSE runs two Class 10 board exams a year from 2026 and is phasing in new NCERT textbooks; Singapore's SEC replaces O- and N-Levels from 2027. US standards and tests vary by state. Check your school's current scheme of work for specifics.

College costs: US figures are College Board 2025–26 averages and BLS 2025 earnings; India and Singapore figures come from published fee schedules for 2025–26 and 2026 intakes. Fees change every year and aid varies by family, so confirm with each institution.
