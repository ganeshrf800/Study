Immunity, vaccines, AIDS and cancer

Your body defends itself in two layers. Innate immunity is the fast, general barrier you are born with. Adaptive immunity is slower on the first meeting with a germ, but it remembers, so the next meeting is far faster and stronger. Vaccines train this memory without the disease.

  • Innate (non-specific): physical barriers (skin, mucus), physiological barriers (acid in stomach, saliva, tears), cellular barriers (neutrophils, monocytes, macrophages, NK cells) and cytokine barriers (interferons).
  • Adaptive (specific, with memory): B-lymphocytes make antibodies (humoral immunity); T-lymphocytes drive cell-mediated immunity and graft rejection.
  • An antibody is a Y-shaped protein of 4 chains: 2 heavy + 2 light (H2L2). Classes: IgA, IgM, IgE, IgG, IgD.
  • Active immunity: your own body makes antibodies (infection, vaccine). Passive: ready-made antibodies are given (colostrum IgA, anti-tetanus or antivenom serum).
  • AIDS: HIV is a retrovirus with RNA genome; it enters helper T-cells (CD4), makes DNA by reverse transcriptase and the T-cell count falls, so the person gets opportunistic infections. Cancer: loss of contact inhibition and uncontrolled division, aided by oncogenes and carcinogens; spread to other sites is metastasis.

In the real world

India's Covid-19 vaccination drive began on 16 January 2021 and crossed 200 crore (2 billion) doses by mid-2022, tracked on the Co-WIN portal. Covishield (a harmless adenovirus carrying the spike gene) and Covaxin (inactivated virus) used the older ideas; mRNA vaccines (Pfizer, Moderna) deliver spike-protein mRNA in lipid particles so your own cells make the antigen. All were given as two doses a few weeks apart, then a booster, exactly the pattern in the graph. Worked example: if R0 = 3, herd immunity threshold = 1 − 1/3 = 0.667, so about 67% must be immune.

Why is the secondary immune response faster and stronger than the primary?

The first exposure creates long-lived memory lymphocytes. On re-exposure they divide and make antibodies within days instead of weeks. This is the principle behind vaccination.
