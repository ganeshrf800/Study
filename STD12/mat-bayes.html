Conditional probability and Bayes' theorem

Conditional probability P(A | B) is the chance of A once you know that B has happened: the sample space shrinks to B. Bayes' theorem runs the conditioning backwards, from an observed effect (a positive test) to a hidden cause (the disease). The result is often surprising, because a rare condition produces many false alarms from the large healthy group.

  • P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B), defined only when P(B) > 0. Rearranged it gives the multiplication rule P(A ∩ B) = P(B) P(A | B).
  • Events A and B are independent when P(A ∩ B) = P(A) P(B), equivalently P(A | B) = P(A). Independent is not the same as mutually exclusive.
  • If E₁, ..., En partition the sample space, the total probability is P(A) = Σ P(Ei) P(A | Ei).
  • Bayes' theorem: P(Ei | A) = P(Ei) P(A | Ei) / Σj P(Ej) P(A | Ej). P(Ei) are priors, P(Ei | A) are posteriors.
  • Counting people instead of multiplying fractions (natural frequencies) makes Bayes' theorem easy to check by hand.

In the real world

Suppose a rapid test for a disease has sensitivity 99% and a false positive rate of 5%, and 1% of a town has the disease. Imagine 10,000 people. About 100 are sick: 99 test positive and 1 is missed. Of the 9,900 healthy people, 5% = 495 test positive by mistake. So 99 + 495 = 594 people test positive, and only 99 of them are sick: P(sick | positive) = 99/594 = 1/6 ≈ 16.7%. By Bayes' theorem: (0.01 × 0.99) / (0.01 × 0.99 + 0.99 × 0.05) = 0.0099 / 0.0594 = 1/6.

A negative result is far more reassuring: 9,405 healthy people test negative against 1 sick, so P(sick | negative) = 1/9,406 ≈ 0.011%. This is why doctors confirm a positive screening result with a second, more specific test, and why screening works best on groups where the prior is higher. A disease affects 2% of people. A test is positive for 90% of sick people and also for 5% of healthy people. A person tests positive. About what is the probability they are sick?

P = (0.02 × 0.90) / (0.02 × 0.90 + 0.98 × 0.05) = 0.018 / 0.067 ≈ 0.269. In 1000 people: 18 sick positives against 49 healthy positives, 18/67.
