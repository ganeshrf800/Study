Ohm's law, drift velocity and resistivity

A current is charge flowing past a point each second. In a metal, free electrons drift very slowly against the field while colliding with ions; these collisions are the resistance. Resistance depends on the wire's material, length, cross-section and temperature.

  • I = Q/t. By convention current is drawn in the direction positive charge would move, opposite to electron flow.
  • Ohm's law V = IR holds for metals at constant temperature. R = ρL/A, where the resistivity ρ is a property of the material only.
  • Drift velocity vd = I/(nAe) is tiny (fractions of a millimetre per second), yet a bulb lights instantly because the field travels at nearly the speed of light.
  • In metals ρ rises with temperature; in semiconductors it falls. Alloys like manganin and constantan have very small α and are used for standard resistors.
  • Series: R = R₁ + R₂ + … (same current). Parallel: 1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + … (same voltage). Power P = VI = I²R = V²/R.

In the real world

Household wiring and fuses. Appliances in a home are connected in parallel across 230 V, so each gets the full voltage and one can be switched off without affecting the others. But their currents add. A 2000 W geyser draws 2000/230 ≈ 8.7 A, a 1500 W air conditioner 6.5 A and a 1000 W iron 4.3 A: together 19.6 A, which would overload a 16 A circuit. The fuse or MCB is the deliberate weak link: a low-melting wire (or magnetic/thermal trip) opens the circuit before the cable overheats. Heavy loads use thicker wire (larger A, so smaller R = ρL/A and less I²R heating), which is why India wires 5 A circuits for lights and fans and 15/16 A circuits for geysers and ACs.

Indian power transmission. Power P = VI, and heat lost in the line is I²R. To send 100 kW through a line of resistance 0.5 Ω, a 440 V supply needs I = 227 A and loses 227² × 0.5 ≈ 25.8 kW (26%). At 11 kV, I = 9.1 A and the loss is only about 41 W. This is why the national grid steps voltage up to 400 kV and 765 kV AC, and uses ±800 kV HVDC for long links such as Raigarh to Pugalur, then steps it down near consumers.
