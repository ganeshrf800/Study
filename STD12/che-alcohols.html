Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers (NCERT Chemistry Part II, Ch 7)

Preparation, acidity, dehydration and the Lucas test

Alcohols (R–OH) and phenols (Ar–OH) contain the hydroxyl group; ethers (R–O–R') have an oxygen bridging two carbons. The O–H bond makes alcohols and phenols weakly acidic and strongly hydrogen-bonded, which is why they boil far higher than ethers of similar mass.

  • Alcohols are made by hydration of alkenes (Markovnikov), hydroboration-oxidation (anti-Markovnikov), reduction of aldehydes, ketones and acids, and by Grignard reagents: HCHO gives a 1° alcohol, other aldehydes 2°, ketones 3°.
  • Phenol is made industrially by the cumene process (benzene + propene gives cumene, which is oxidised and cleaved to phenol + acetone), and in the lab from benzenediazonium chloride.
  • Acidity: carboxylic acids > phenols > water > 1° > 2° > 3° alcohols. The phenoxide ion is stabilised by resonance; electron-withdrawing groups (–NO2) at o/p positions increase acidity, electron-donating groups (–CH3) decrease it.
  • Dehydration with acid: ease 3° > 2° > 1°, following carbocation stability. Ethanol with conc. H2SO4 gives ethoxyethane at 413 K (excess alcohol) and ethene at 443 K (excess acid).
  • Lucas reagent (anhydrous ZnCl2 in conc. HCl) separates the three classes by how fast the insoluble alkyl chloride makes the solution turbid: 3° at once, 2° in about 5 minutes, 1° not at room temperature.

In the real world

India's E20 programme blends 20 % ethanol (made by fermenting sugarcane juice, molasses and grain) into petrol. In 1000 L of E20 there are 200 L ethanol and 800 L petrol. With energy contents of roughly 32 MJ/L for petrol and 21 MJ/L for ethanol, the blend carries 0.8 × 32 + 0.2 × 21 = 29.8 MJ/L, about 7 % less than pure petrol, which is why fuel economy dips slightly. Aspirin is a phenol derivative: salicylic acid (M = 138 g/mol; made by the Kolbe reaction) is acetylated with acetic anhydride to give aspirin (M = 180 g/mol). 13.8 g (0.100 mol) of salicylic acid can give at most 0.100 × 180 = 18.0 g aspirin. Methanol is toxic (it is oxidised to methanal and methanoic acid in the body), which is why illicit liquor contaminated with it causes tragedies, and why industrial ethanol is denatured.

In the Lucas test, which alcohol gives turbidity within about 5 minutes at room temperature?

Propan-2-ol is a secondary alcohol: turbidity appears in about 5 minutes. The tertiary alcohol turns turbid immediately, while primary alcohols (ethanol, methanol) show no turbidity at room temperature.
