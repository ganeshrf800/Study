Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants

Flower, pollination and double fertilisation

A flower is the reproductive organ of an angiosperm. Pollen from the anther reaches the stigma (pollination), a pollen tube carries two male gametes to the ovule, and both are used: one fuses with the egg and one with the two polar nuclei. This double fertilisation is found only in flowering plants.

  • Four whorls: calyx (sepals), corolla (petals), androecium (stamens, male), gynoecium (carpels, female). A pistil = stigma + style + ovary.
  • Pollination: autogamy (same flower), geitonogamy (another flower, same plant), xenogamy (different plant, brings genetic variation). Agents: wind, water, insects, birds, bats.
  • A mature embryo sac (female gametophyte) is 7-celled and 8-nucleate: egg + 2 synergids (egg apparatus), 3 antipodals, and a large central cell with 2 polar nuclei. A mature pollen grain has a vegetative cell and a generative cell; the generative cell divides to give 2 male gametes.
  • Double fertilisation: syngamy (male gamete + egg gives zygote, 2n) and triple fusion (male gamete + 2 polar nuclei gives primary endosperm nucleus, 3n).
  • After fertilisation: zygote becomes embryo, PEN becomes endosperm, ovule becomes seed, ovary becomes fruit, ovary wall becomes pericarp. Apple and strawberry are false fruits because the thalamus also contributes.

In the real world

Rice is self-pollinated, so Indian breeders at IARI and other institutes make hybrids by hand: they emasculate the bud of the female parent, bag it, dust it with pollen from the chosen male parent and bag it again. Rice has 2n = 24. Pollen grain n = 12, egg n = 12, so the zygote has 12 + 12 = 24 and the endosperm (the white grain we cook) has 12 + 12 + 12 = 36 chromosomes. Mango is cross-pollinated, and the varieties Amrapali (Dashehari × Neelum) and Mallika (Neelum × Dashehari) were produced by the same hand-pollination method.

A plant has pollen grains with n = 10 chromosomes. What is the chromosome number in its primary endosperm nucleus?

PEN forms by triple fusion: two polar nuclei (n each) + one male gamete (n) = 3n = 30. The zygote is 2n = 20.
