Vector Algebra

Vectors: dot product and cross product

A vector has a magnitude and a direction, and in three dimensions it is written with three components. The dot product turns two vectors into a number that measures how much they point the same way (and gives angles and projections). The cross product turns them into a new vector perpendicular to both, whose length is the area of the parallelogram they span.

  • Vector a = a₁i + a₂j + a₃k has magnitude |a| = √(a₁² + a₂² + a₃²) and unit vector â = a/|a|. Its direction cosines are a₁/|a|, a₂/|a|, a₃/|a|.
  • Dot product a·b = |a||b| cos θ = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ + a₃b₃ is a scalar. It is 0 exactly when the (non-zero) vectors are perpendicular; it is commutative.
  • Cross product a×b has magnitude |a||b| sin θ and direction given by the right-hand rule. It is anticommutative: b×a = −(a×b), and a×b = 0 for parallel vectors.
  • |a×b| is the area of the parallelogram on a and b; ½|a×b| is the area of the triangle. The scalar triple product a·(b×c) is the volume of the parallelepiped on a, b, c.
  • i×j = k, j×k = i, k×i = j, and i·i = j·j = k·k = 1, i·j = 0.

In the real world

A drone's flight controller adds vectors all the time. Suppose its velocity through the air is v = (8, 6, 2) m/s (east, north, up) and the wind is w = (−3, 4, 0) m/s. Its velocity over the ground is v + w = (5, 10, 2) m/s, with speed √(25 + 100 + 4) = √129 ≈ 11.36 m/s. It climbs at sin⁻¹(2/11.36) ≈ 10.1° and its ground track is tan⁻¹(5/10) ≈ 26.6° east of north.

The dot product shows something neat here: w·v = (−3)(8) + (4)(6) + 0·2 = 0, so the wind is exactly perpendicular to the air-velocity vector and neither helps nor hinders the drone along its heading. The cross product v × w = (6·0 − 2·4, 2·(−3) − 8·0, 8·4 − 6·(−3)) = (−8, −6, 50) points almost straight up, with |v × w| = √(64 + 36 + 2500) ≈ 51.0 the area of the parallelogram the two vectors span.

For what value of λ are the vectors i + 2j + 3k and λi − j + k perpendicular?

Perpendicular means a·b = 0: (1)(λ) + (2)(−1) + (3)(1) = λ − 2 + 3 = λ + 1 = 0, so λ = −1.
