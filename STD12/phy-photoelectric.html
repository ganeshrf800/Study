Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter

Photoelectric effect and de Broglie waves

Light shone on a metal can knock electrons out, but only if its frequency is high enough. Einstein explained this by treating light as packets called photons, each of energy E = hν. De Broglie then turned the idea around: if waves can behave as particles, particles such as electrons should have a wavelength.

  • Below the threshold frequency ν0 there is no emission, however bright the light. Above it, emission is instantaneous.
  • Intensity decides how many photons arrive, so the saturation current is proportional to intensity. Frequency decides the maximum kinetic energy, so the stopping potential depends on frequency only.
  • Einstein's equation: hν = φ + Kmax, where φ = hν0 is the work function. The graph of V0 against ν is a straight line with slope h/e.
  • Every moving particle has a wavelength λ = h/p. It is measurable for electrons and invisible for a cricket ball.

In the real world

Photocells in automatic street lights and door sensors, and the light meter that sets exposure in a camera, rely on the photoelectric effect. Worked case: violet light of λ = 400 nm falls on potassium (φ = 2.30 eV). Photon energy = 1240/400 = 3.10 eV, so Kmax = 3.10 − 2.30 = 0.80 eV and V0 = 0.80 V; the threshold wavelength is 1240/2.30 = 539 nm, so red light does nothing. Matter waves are used in electron microscopes at Indian labs such as IISc and AIIMS: electrons accelerated through 100 V have λ = 1.227/√100 = 0.123 nm, about 4000 times shorter than visible light, so they resolve details of atomic size. A 150 g cricket ball at 30 m/s has λ = 6.63×10-34/(0.15 × 30) = 1.5×10-34 m, far too small to detect, which is why we never see cricket balls diffract.

The intensity of light falling on a metal surface is doubled, with frequency kept above the threshold. Which statement is correct?

Doubling intensity doubles the number of photons per second and so the number of electrons per second (saturation current). Each photon still has energy hν, so Kmax and V0 are unchanged.
