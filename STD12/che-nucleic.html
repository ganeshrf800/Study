Nucleic acids, base pairing and vitamins

DNA and RNA are polymers of nucleotides, each made of a sugar, a phosphate and a nitrogenous base. The order of bases stores genetic information, and hydrogen bonding between specific base pairs lets DNA copy itself and be read. Vitamins are small organic molecules the body needs in tiny amounts but cannot make in enough quantity.

  • Nucleoside = base + sugar; nucleotide = base + sugar + phosphate. Nucleotides are joined by phosphodiester links between C3' of one sugar and C5' of the next.
  • DNA: sugar is 2-deoxyribose; bases A, G (purines), C, T (pyrimidines); double helix of two antiparallel strands. RNA: sugar is ribose; T is replaced by uracil (U); usually single-stranded (mRNA, rRNA, tRNA).
  • Base pairing: A pairs with T (2 hydrogen bonds), G pairs with C (3 hydrogen bonds). Chargaff's rule: in DNA, A = T and G = C.
  • DNA replicates semi-conservatively; during protein synthesis the code is copied into mRNA, which is read three bases (one codon) at a time.
  • Vitamins: fat-soluble A, D, E, K are stored in the body; water-soluble B-complex and C must be supplied regularly. Each deficiency has its own disease.

In the real world

A human haploid genome has about 3.1 × 10⁹ base pairs. At 0.34 nm per base pair that is 3.1 × 10⁹ × 0.34 nm = 1.05 × 10⁹ nm, about 1.05 m of DNA, so a diploid cell (two sets) holds roughly 2 m of DNA packed into a nucleus a few micrometres wide. The same base-pair uniqueness is behind DNA fingerprinting used in Indian forensic and paternity cases. A 10-base-pair stretch with 4 G–C and 6 A–T pairs has 4 × 3 + 6 × 2 = 24 hydrogen bonds. Edible oils such as mustard, groundnut and sunflower oil are triglycerides of fatty acids and carry vitamin E; FSSAI-standard fortified oil has vitamins A and D added to help close deficiency gaps, and vitamin D itself is made in our skin on sunlight exposure.

A double-stranded DNA sample has 30 % adenine. What percentage is guanine?

A = T = 30 %, so A + T = 60 %. The remaining 40 % is G + C, and G = C, so G = 20 %.
